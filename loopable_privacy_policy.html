*PRIVACY POLICY FOR LOOPABLE*

Last Updated: September 19, 2026

Golden Controller Tech ("we", "us", "our") operates Loopable, a social networking application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

*1. Information We Collect*

a) Account Information: When you register with email and password, we collect your email address, display name, username, and password (encrypted). When you use Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and profile image as provided by Google.

b) Content You Provide: Posts, comments, likes, follows, and profile information you create.

c) Automatic Information: Device type, operating system, IP address, app version, crash logs. For advertising, we use Google AdMob which may collect Google Advertising ID.

d) We do not collect sensitive personal data, financial data, location data, or video at this time.

*2. How We Use Your Information*

- To create and manage your account and authenticate login

- To provide the social feed, display posts, and enable following

- To maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse

- To show ads via AdMob and measure app performance

- To communicate updates and support

*3. Legal Basis (for Nigeria and other regions)*

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, we process personal data based on: your consent, performance of contract (providing the Loopable service), legitimate interests (security, improvement), and compliance with law.

*4. Sharing of Information*

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

- Google (for Google Sign-In and AdMob advertising)

- Service providers for hosting and analytics who are bound by confidentiality

- Legal authorities when required by Nigerian or US law

Our app may contain links to third parties. Their policies govern their sites.

*5. Data Retention*

We keep your account data as long as your account is active. You can delete your account inside the app under Settings > Delete Account. Upon deletion, we delete or anonymize your posts and personal data within 30 days, except where we must retain for legal purposes.

*6. Data Security and Storage*

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect data including encrypted passwords and secure transmission. Data is stored on secure cloud servers and may be processed in Nigeria, the United States, and other countries where our service providers operate. We take steps to ensure adequate protection for cross-border transfers as required by NDPA.

*7. Your Rights*

*For Users in Nigeria (NDPA 2023):*

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object to processing, data portability, and withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

*For Users in the United States:*

- California residents (CCPA/CPRA) have the right to know what data we collect, to delete data, to opt-out of sale/sharing (we do not sell data), and to non-discrimination.

- We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us to delete it (COPPA compliance).

- To exercise rights, email us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days, or 45 days for US requests as allowed by law.

To opt-out of personalized advertising, you can reset your Advertising ID in your device settings or enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

*8. Children's Privacy*

Loopable is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Users under 18 require parental consent as per NDPA.

- Google AdMob - https://policies.google.com/privacy
